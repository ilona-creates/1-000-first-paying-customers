Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy covers the waitlist for First 1,000 Paying Customers, currently in pre-launch. It's written in plain language on purpose — if anything is unclear, email me and I'll clarify it directly.

I'm Ilona, and this is the privacy policy for this website (the "Site"), where you can join the waitlist for my course, First 1,000 Paying Customers. This policy explains what information I collect when you visit the Site or join the waitlist, why I collect it, and what rights you have over it.

Who's responsible for your data

I'm Ilona Melnychuk, and I run this Site and the course as an individual. For any privacy question, you can reach me directly at ilona@ilonamelnychuk.com.

What I collect

When you join the waitlist, I collect:

  • Your name, as you enter it in the form
  • Your email address
  • The date and time you signed up

I don't ask for or store any payment information at this stage — there's nothing to buy yet. If you simply browse the Site without submitting the form, I don't collect any personal information about you. The Site does not use cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising trackers.

Like any website, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may automatically log standard technical information for security and reliability purposes, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request. I don't have access to this beyond aggregate, anonymised statistics, if any.

Why I collect it

I use your name and email address to:

  • Add you to the waitlist and confirm your spot
  • Email you when the course opens for enrolment, and about related updates (for example, early-bird access)
  • Occasionally share content related to the course topic, if I think it will be useful to you

I will never sell your details, and I won't share them with anyone for marketing purposes that aren't related to this course.

Where your data is stored

Waitlist submissions are stored in a private Google Sheet, using Google's infrastructure (Google Workspace / Google Apps Script) as my data processor. Google may process this data on servers outside your country of residence, under its own security and privacy commitments. I don't use any other third party to store or process your waitlist submission at this time.

The Site's fonts are served by Google Fonts. Loading a page may request font files from Google's servers, which can involve your IP address being visible to Google as part of that request.

If and when the course itself launches, enrolment and payment will likely be handled through a third-party course or payment platform (for example, a platform like Podia). That platform will have its own privacy policy governing any purchase, and I'll point you to it clearly before you pay for anything.

Legal basis for processing (EU/UK visitors)

If you're in the UK or EU, my legal basis for processing your name and email address is your consent — you give this by submitting the waitlist form. You can withdraw that consent at any time (see "Your rights" below).

How long I keep it

I keep waitlist entries until the course has launched and you've either enrolled or told me you're no longer interested, or until you ask me to delete your data — whichever comes first. If the course doesn't go ahead, I'll delete the waitlist entirely.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data I hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Ask me to delete your data
  • Withdraw consent and be removed from the waitlist at any time
  • Object to or restrict how I use your data
  • Complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO) if you think I've mishandled your data

To exercise any of these, just email ilona@ilonamelnychuk.com — I'll action it within a reasonable timeframe, and in any case within 30 days.

Children's privacy

This Site and course are intended for adult founders and business owners. I don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

As the course moves from waitlist to launch, this policy will need to grow with it — for example, once payments are involved. I'll update this page when that happens and update the "Last updated" date above. For material changes, I'll email the waitlist directly.

Contact

Questions, requests, or just want to double-check something? Email me at ilona@ilonamelnychuk.com.
